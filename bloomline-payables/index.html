How the three-way match works
    
    
      
  • SSupplier statement: what the farm says you owe. KKometSales: what your books say. PProof of payment: what you can prove you paid.
    • 
      
  • Invoices are matched by invoice number between the statement and Komet; if the numbers differ, a same-amount invoice within 5 days counts as a match with a "Ref differs" warning.
    • 
      
  • Payments are matched across all three by amount, within 10 days or by reference.
    • 
      
  • Reconciled is the best-evidence balance: every invoice either side knows about (Komet's amount wins when they differ), minus credits, minus every payment found in any source. The bridge on the match channel shows exactly how each statement balance gets there.
    • 
      
  • Upload Komet data and statements that cover the same period. Anything dated after a supplier's latest statement shows as pending, not as an error.
